Hello there — I'm Diego Campos, a performance tester and full‑stack tinkerer from Sevilla. I measure, break, and fix software so users never have to (that’s my day job, old sport). I build scalable performance suites with JMeter, experiment with k6 and Playwright for modern automation, and wire tests into CI with Jenkins and Docker. 

By night I practice French (oui, j'adore le français 🇫🇷) and polish my already excellent English — truly spectacular, old sport. I'm respectful, a little playful, and I like to keep things light: if a test fails it might just be me making sure your app is ready for anything. 😄

If you'd like to collaborate, review a performance report, or share a good croissant spot in Sevilla, connect with me on GitHub or LinkedIn — let's build something that survives real traffic. ❤️
